# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 20, 2026_

Tikist is built to keep your data yours. We do not collect it.

## What we collect

Nothing. Tikist collects no personal data. Your tasks, notes, and attachments stay on your device, and sync across your own devices through your personal iCloud account if you enable it. We run no servers and never receive your data.

## Camera & photos

If you attach a photo to a task, Tikist may request camera access. Photos stay on your device and in your iCloud. They are never sent to us or anyone else. The app works fully without this permission.

## Third parties

Tikist includes one open-source, on-device image cropper (TOCropViewController). It has no network access and collects nothing. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Your control

Delete the app and clear its iCloud data anytime to remove everything. We hold no copy.
