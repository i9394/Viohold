How to Batch-Save Multiple Images from One Webpage on iPhone & iPad

Stop long-pressing 15 photos one by one in Safari. Filter out tiny logos and avatars by pixel size and save selected gallery images together.

Applies to: iOS 18+ & iPadOS 18+ Updated: November 2026

Saving a single photo in Safari is easy. Saving 15 reference photos, design assets, or gallery images from one webpage is tedious—you have to long-press each image one by one, wait for the menu, tap Save, and hope you did not miss any.

Built-in iOS Options vs. Dedicated Batch Image Selection

  • Safari + Drag-and-Drop / Shortcuts: Works on basic pages, but often grabs tiny icons, avatars, and low-resolution thumbnails unless you manually inspect every item.
  • Cove Browser Image Selection: Cove Browser scans the currently loaded webpage for detected images and presents them in a clean selection grid.

How It Works in Cove Browser

  1. Open the webpage in Cove Browser and let the gallery load.
  2. Press and hold any image on the page and choose Save Multiple Images (or choose Save Image to save just that single image for free).
  3. Use the minimum pixel size filter to automatically hide tiny site logos, icons, and UI buttons.
  4. Select the exact images you want (or select all) and tap Save to store them in Cove's organized Files library, where you can preview, rename, or share them to Photos.

Free vs. Pro & Limitations

  • Single image saving is free. Multi-image batch selection requires Cove Pro ($2.99/month or $14.99/year with a 3-day free trial).
  • Cove only saves images detected on the currently loaded webpage and does not bypass logins, paywalls, or protected platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save multiple images from a webpage at once on iPhone?

Open the webpage in Cove Browser, press and hold any image, choose Save Multiple Images, filter out small icons by minimum pixel size, select the images you want, and tap Save.

Is image saving free in Cove Browser?

Saving individual images one at a time is free. Batch-selecting and saving multiple webpage images at once is part of Cove Pro ($2.99/month or $14.99/year).
