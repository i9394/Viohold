Transparency & Supported Scope

  • Cove Browser supports standard, publicly playable MP4 and HLS (.m3u8) web videos and images on supported websites where you have permission to save them.
  • Not supported: YouTube, Netflix, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), Vimeo, DRM-protected streams, built-in VPN, and background audio playback.
