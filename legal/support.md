## Frequently Asked Questions

### Ad Blocking

**Some ads still show up on a page. What can I do?**
Cove's filtering engine covers most banners, pop-ups, floating bars, redirects, and trackers, but no filter catches everything. First try **Settings → Ad Filter → Update Ad Rules** to fetch the latest rule set. For a stubborn element, tap the shield icon in the address bar and use **Hide Distracting Item** to remove it manually. If a site keeps showing ads, send us the page URL via Settings → About → Feedback so we can improve the rules.

**A page looks broken or a button does not respond after filtering.**
Occasionally a filter rule removes an element the page needs. Open **Settings → Ad Filter → Filter Rules**, turn off the matching rule (Banner / Pop-up / Floating / Redirect Blocking), and refresh the page. You can also add the site to **Site Whitelist** to disable filtering on that site only.

**How do I enable the Safari content blocker?**
Open **Settings → Ad Filter → Safari Content Blocking** and tap **Enable**; this takes you to iOS Settings where you can turn on the Cove extension. Rules are shared with Safari once enabled.

### Privacy & App Lock

**How do I lock the app?**
Open **Settings → Privacy & Security → Set Passcode**. After a passcode is set, you can turn on **Face ID Unlock** for quicker access. Authentication is required whenever you return to the app from the background.

**What happens to my content when I switch apps?**
When Cove moves to the background, its content is covered so it does not appear in the app switcher.

**Where is my browsing data stored?**
Browsing data stays on your device and is never uploaded. You can clear history anytime from the **Browsing History** page (tap **Clear**).

### Files & Offline Use

**Where can I find files I saved?**
Open the **Files** tab. Saved items are sorted automatically into videos, images, and documents.

**Can I bring in files from other apps?**
Yes. Use the iOS share sheet from any app and choose Cove to import the file into your library.

**Why can't I save resources on some websites?**
To respect copyright, Cove maintains a domain blocklist covering major content platforms. Saving is not available on those pages. See our Terms of Use for details.

**Why is saving slower on the free tier?**
The free tier processes one task at a time. Cove Pro enables simultaneous tasks and higher per-task throughput.

### Media Player & Conversion

**Which formats can the player handle?**
The built-in player supports common video and audio formats, including MP4, MOV, and M4V. TS files are converted to MP4 automatically so they play smoothly.

**What does "format conversion" do?**
Cove Pro can re-encode a file to HEVC (H.265) to reduce its size, or to a compatibility profile so it plays on older devices.

**How do I use the enhanced player?**
Turn it on in **Settings → Playback Settings → Enhanced Player**. It adds swipe gestures for brightness, volume, and seeking, long-press for playback speed, remembered playback position, and volume normalization across videos. The enhanced player is part of Cove Pro.

### Cove Pro & Subscriptions

**How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?**
Open the app → **Settings → Cove Pro → Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

**How do I cancel a subscription?**
Open iOS **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Cove Pro → Cancel Subscription**. Your Pro features remain active until the end of the current billing period.

**Will I be charged automatically?**
Yes. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.
